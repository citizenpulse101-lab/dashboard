## Why CitizenPulse?

Traditional polls often suffer from:
- Small sample sizes
- High self-censorship
- Regional bias
- Slow reporting

**CitizenPulse** analyzes **real conversations at scale** in real time — delivering faster, larger, and more authentic citizen sentiment.
