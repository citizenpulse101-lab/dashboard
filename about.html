The Problem We're Solving

An independent, citizen-owned mirror of public sentiment — contrasting what people actually say on X against narratives, official claims, and government statements.

Traditional polls are broken

Phone-based surveys suffer declining response rates. Government funding creates pressure. Question framing shapes outcomes. Sample sizes rarely exceed 45% approval thresholds.

Media coverage is selective

Official narratives dominate while citizen voices get amplified, re-interpreted, or ignored. Real-time Arab, Hebrew, Farsi, and English voices are often missing.

Governments misrepresent publics

Leaders claim Arab publics are 54% in favor of normalization. They often use under 45% approval ratings.

The citizen voice is the missing data

Billions of people speaking daily on X — the largest unrestricted source of real-world sentiment — is largely ignored by policy researchers.

Our Solution — X + Grok AI

CitizenPulse sources data directly from the X API — millions of public posts in Arabic, Hebrew, English, Farsi, French, and more. We process them through Grok AI for real-time sentiment scoring. No phone surveys. No government funding. No hidden bias.
