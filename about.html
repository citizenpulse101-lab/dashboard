The Problem We're Solving

An independent, citizen-owned mirror of public sentiment — contrasting what people actually say on X against official narratives, media claims, and government statements.

Traditional polls are broken

Low response rates, government funding bias, leading questions, and small unrepresentative samples.

Media coverage is selective

Official narratives dominate while real citizen voices in Arabic, Hebrew, Farsi and English are often ignored or reinterpreted.

Governments misrepresent publics

Leaders frequently claim public support that does not match real conversations happening on X.

The citizen voice is missing

Billions of daily authentic posts on X — the largest open public square — are largely ignored by traditional research.

Our Solution — X + Grok AI

CitizenPulse sources data directly from the X API — millions of public posts in multiple languages. We process them through Grok AI for real-time, unbiased sentiment analysis. No phone surveys. No government funding. No editorial filter.

Our Mission

To systematically under-represent citizen voices through self-censorship, editorial bias, or political pressure. CitizenPulse is the missing half of the picture — giving power back to real public opinion.

Independent • Transparent • Citizen-First • Powered by X Public Data + Grok AI
